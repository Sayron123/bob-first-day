# Step 7 — Handoff

Write `.bob/tickets/<slug>/05-handoff.md` with two short sections:

## For the client (plain language, 3–5 lines)
- What changed, in their words (no file names, no jargon).
- Where to see it.
- Anything they should know or decide next.

## For the developer (5–8 lines)
- Files changed (list).
- Checks run and results.
- Known limits / follow-ups.
- Link to `02-map.md` for the full before/after.

Finish the task with a one-line summary in chat. Keep all chat replies
brief; the files hold the detail.
